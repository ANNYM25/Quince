What a judge will remember

The ten best things in the final strategy

The governing line

"Quince pays rented platforms to re-buy customers it already has." Diagnosis and answer in one sentence.

The trust thesis

The first order buys trust; the second spends it. It explains why customers don't come back, not only what to do.

The A5 correction

Counting only engagement after the first order, app users still repeat at 21.5%. The obvious objection becomes the proof.

Owned undervalued 3×

Attribution IQ: owned channels take part in 15.0% of revenue but get 4.8% of last-touch credit.

The Golden Month, measured

Engaged buyers return at 5.6% in week 2 vs about 1% for non-engaged; half their returns land in weeks 1–4.

Retargeting = organic

23.0% vs 23.4% repeat, and its visitors convert below organic (4.85% vs 5.08%).

Quince Passport

The box is the one owned channel that reaches 100% of buyers. Every parcel becomes a pop-up store inside the customer's home.

Who and when

Twelve CJA segments with timing, channel and message. Owned first; paid only where owned can't reach.

Gates both ways

No money moves until CJA proves it, and failed tranches go back. The data was allowed to limit the plan.

~$25M from the same $120M

+$15.8M from repeat and ~170K extra new customers a year, with every formula shown.
